# Lab 4 Marking Guide - 100 Points

## Session 1 - 60 points

Session 1 contains 27 embedded questions:

- Q1, Q2, Q12, Q16, Q18, and Q26: 3 points each
- All remaining Session 1 questions: 2 points each

Marking for a 3-point question:

- 3: correct, complete, technically justified, and linked to the laboratory evidence
- 2: mostly correct but incomplete or weakly justified
- 1: limited partial understanding
- 0: missing, incorrect, or contradicted by the evidence

Marking for a 2-point question:

- 2: correct and sufficiently specific to the laboratory
- 1: partly correct or incomplete
- 0: missing or incorrect

Required outputs are evidence for awarding the associated question marks. Unsupported answers may receive reduced credit.

## Session 2 - 40 points

Session 2 contains 20 embedded questions at 2 points each:

- 2: correct, concise, and supported by the uploaded Pi evidence
- 1: partly correct or incomplete
- 0: missing, incorrect, or unsupported

## Official submissions

- Session 1 completed Colab PDF: 60 points
- Session 2 completed Colab PDF: 40 points

The fork URL and Session 1 commit ID must be present in both PDFs.
